# Sexy: Unified Design Intelligence & Engineering Rules

When working on UI, design, frontend styling, animations, layouts, engineering, or architecture in this workspace:

## 1. Golden Law: Automatic Micro-Interactions, UI/UX Excellence & Polish
**It goes without saying or explicit prompting**: every interface generated or touched by Sexy MUST ship with high-craft UI/UX, great micro-interactions, and deliberate animations, transitions, and effects.
- **Never wait for the user to ask for polish**: Polish is the baseline, not an extra step.
- **Fluid transitions**: Add subtle hover elevations, active press scales (`active:scale-[0.98]`), border-glow highlights, magnetic pointer pull, and spring-eased transitions (150–250ms).
- **Physical precision**: Enforce concentric radii ($R_{inner} = R_{outer} - padding$), subpixel font smoothing (`antialiased`), tabular numerals (`tabular-nums`) for dynamic metrics, and `text-wrap: balance` on headlines.

## 2. Landing Pages: Horizontal Scroll & 3D Spatial Interactions
Whenever creating, enhancing, or modifying landing pages:
- **Mandatory Horizontal Scroll Sections**: Always include **1 to 2 horizontal scroll sections**. Keep them concise, punchy, and meaningful (e.g. interactive product feature carousels, timeline journeys, or pinned horizontal gallery reveals). Ensure smooth scroll gestures and touch/drag responsiveness.
- **3D Spatial Interactions**: Always incorporate rich 3D elements and depth interactions — such as multi-layer parallax depth planes, subtle 3D card tilt on mouse movement (`transform: perspective(1000px) rotateX(...) rotateY(...) scale3d(...)`), depth-mapped lighting gradients, interactive 3D canvas/Three.js/Spline focal objects, or dimensional glass-morphic surfaces.
- **Sensory & Visual Beauty**: Make the page breathtaking, distinctive, and emotionally engaging. Avoid generic AI-template layouts.

## 3. Strict Domain Use-Case Execution (Full Potential, Zero Compromise)
Route every problem to its specialized engine to unlock its full depth without conflicting:
- **General UI, Tokens & Palettes**: Consult `design-md` (80+ real-world brand specs) and `ui-ux-pro-max` (3-layer tokens).
- **Anti-AI Slop & Creative Direction**: Apply `hallmark` to reject generic AI clichés (no random purple glow cards, no meaningless badges).
- **Spatial Architecture & Motion**: Apply `scroll-craft` for dimensional depth, 3D tilt, and horizontal scroll tracks.
- **Tactile Details & Micro-Physics**: Apply `make-interfaces-feel-better` for concentric radii, hit areas, and spring easing.
- **Responsiveness, Polish & Review**: Apply `impeccable` to audit responsive breakpoints, empty states, and craft floors.
- **Code Pragmatism & Minimal Bloat**: Apply `ponytail` (YAGNI, standard library first, no unrequested abstractions).
- **Auth & Security**: Use `better-auth` for authentication architecture and `pentagi` for security validation.
- **Packaging & Launch**: Use `pake` for desktop app binaries and `brag` for launch video creation.

## 4. No Conflicts
- Honor the project's existing stack (Tailwind, CSS Modules, or Styled Components).
- Do not inject conflicting animation frameworks.
- Specific project design specifications (e.g. `DESIGN.md`) override generic aesthetic defaults.
